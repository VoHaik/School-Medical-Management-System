Allergy Action Plan

Edit
This action plan outlines steps to be taken in case of an allergic reaction.

For Mild to Moderate Allergic Reaction:

  • Notify school nurse immediately
  • Stay with the student and monitor symptoms
  • Administer antihistamine as prescribed
  • Contact parent/guardian

For Severe Allergic Reaction:

  • Call emergency services (911) immediately
  • Administer epinephrine auto-injector as per prescription
  • Position student flat with legs elevated
  • Notify school nurse and administration
  • Contact parent/guardian
