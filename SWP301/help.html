Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new student to the system?

To add a new student, go to Health Records and click "Add New Record". Fill in the required information including personal details, emergency contacts, and medical history. Save the record to complete the process.

Can I set up automatic medication reminders?

Yes, you can set up automatic reminders in the Medications section. When adding or editing a medication, enable notifications and set your preferred reminder schedule.

How do I export health reports?

Navigate to the Reports section, select your desired date range and filters, then use the export buttons to download reports in PDF, Excel, or CSV format.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email address. You'll receive instructions to reset your password via email.

How do I update emergency contact information?

Go to Emergency Contacts section, find the contact you want to update, and click the edit button. Make your changes and save to update the information.
